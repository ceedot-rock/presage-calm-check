Before we switch the camera on

This page reads your breathing rate, pulse, and heart-rate variability from your webcam, using the Presage SmartSpectra SDK, and uses them only to set the pace of the lesson: slower and simpler when your breathing quickens, moving on when you are settled.

  • Your video never leaves this machine. Frames are processed in memory on this computer and never recorded, uploaded, or stored.
  • No health claims. These are study-pace signals, not a medical device. Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or scores your health.
  • You are in control. Stop any time — the camera switches off immediately and the session summary is kept only in this page's memory.
  • Telemetry is off. The SDK's optional session-summary telemetry is disabled in this app.

By starting, you consent to camera use for this session only. You can also try the demo, which uses a clearly labelled simulated signal and no camera at all.

Live mode needs a Presage API key on the server (PRESAGE_API_KEY). The demo below works without one.
